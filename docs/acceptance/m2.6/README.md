# M2.6 "Live-Testing Fix Pass" — verification notes

M2.5's evidence was a screenshot per step, taken by driving the real UI in headless Chromium.
M2.6 fixes eight things found by *playing* that build, and most of them are not visible in a
still frame: a fog estimator, a load order, an audio graph, an actuator ramp, a transmission
gate, a heightfield filter. So this pass is verified two ways:

- **headlessly, with numbers** — `npm test` (106 unit tests over the pure models) plus the
  measurements recorded below, all reproducible from the checked-in source and the *shipped*
  asset payloads;
- **by eye, in a real browser** — the checklist at the bottom. The sandbox has no GPU, no
  reachable Poly Haven CDN and no reachable asset source host, so the visual/audio half cannot
  be evidenced from here.

```sh
npm test          # 106 pass
npx vite build    # clean
```

## 1 — Fog: the sun no longer decides the horizon colour

`horizonRadiance()` on a synthetic equirect image with 8 sun pixels at 20 000 nits sitting on a
160-pixel horizon band (`tests/fog.test.js`):

| estimator | luminance of the estimate |
| --- | --- |
| plain mean (M2.5) | **> 20** — near-white, and 24× the bloom threshold of 0.82 |
| trimmed, sun-rejecting (M2.6) | **< 1.2**, hue preserved (blue-warm, `b > r`) |
| median | **< 1.2** |

The cap scales the colour down rather than clamping channels, so the hue ratios survive
(`4 : 4.4` stays `4 : 4.4`), and every track declares one below the bloom threshold:
`test-circuit-01` 0.6, `forest-dirt-trail` 0.48, `forest-free-roam` 0.46.
`measureHorizon()` returns the *uncapped* estimate; the track's own `fogCap` is applied by
`updateFog()`, so an open circuit can haze brighter than a closed forest.

## 2 — Load order: the selected car first, the sky last

`game.init()` now runs `loadRigFor(carPreset)` → `selectTrack()` → `environment.start()`.
`createEnvironment({ defer: true })` builds the generated canvas sky immediately (so the scene is
never unlit) and only requests the HDRI when `start()` is called. Verified by code path; the
visible proof is the browser network waterfall — see the checklist.

The SUV's texture quality moves 80 → 85 in `scripts/assets/config.mjs`. **This needs a pipeline
re-run to reach `public/`** (see "What must land via CI"). Headroom: the shipped SUV uses
1.47 MB of a 9 MB texture budget and 2.80 MB of an 8 MB file budget, so q85 is not near a limit.

## 3 — Audio: per-cylinder engines, and `setCar()` no longer mutes the game

`setCar()` used to clear `layers` and set `started = false`; only `unlock()` rebuilt them, and
`unlock()` is a user-gesture-only entry point that is never called a second time. It now calls
`buildEngine()` itself when a context already exists, guarded by a load token so two rapid swaps
cannot leave two engines running. If `engine.index.json` cannot be fetched, `FALLBACK_SPECS` still
describes the engine, so the synthesised pulse train runs and **a car can never be silent because
a WAV 404'd**.

Beds regenerated by `scripts/assets/build-audio.mjs` (pure synthesis, ~0.25 s):

| engine | cylinders | firing order | beds (reference RPM → loop) | firing frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aurora-v12` | 12 | 1-7-5-11-3-9-6-12-2-8-4-10 | 1400 → 0.800 s, 4200 → 0.700 s, 8200 → 0.700 s | 140 / 420 / 820 Hz |
| `sikandar-diesel` | 4 | 1-3-4-2 | 900 → 1.000 s, 2100 → 1.000 s, 4400 → 1.002 s | 30 / 70 / 146.7 Hz |

Every loop spans a whole number of firing cycles, so it is seamless when pitched by
`playbackRate = rpm / referenceRpm`.

Runtime synth, measured from the pure helpers (`tests/audio-engine.test.js`):

- **Per-cylinder pulses.** One oscillator per cylinder at `rpm / 120` (one power stroke per two
  revolutions), so the bank sums to the firing frequencies above. The stagger is baked into each
  wave's Fourier phase: the test reconstructs the rotated wave and compares it to the unrotated one
  sampled at `t - tau`, matching to < 1e-9. A `start()` offset is a fixed number of *seconds* and
  would drift out of phase every time the RPM changed.
- **Exhaust resonance** stays near the pipes' own tuning — 109/247/635 Hz at idle to 142/322/828 Hz
  at 8600 rpm for the V12, 51/116/252 to ~75/172/372 Hz for the diesel. Tracking RPM outright would
  make the pipes a second oscillator and lose the "sings at some revs, quiet at others" behaviour.
- **RPM crossfade** of the three beds sums to 1.00 ± 0.02 across 200–12 000 rpm, and the mid bed
  peaks between its neighbours.
- **Shift dip** trough 0.28 at 60 ms, fully recovered by ~300 ms, no sample-to-sample jump above
  0.05 — driven from the exported `shiftDipEnvelope()`, so the shape the test asserts is the shape
  that is heard.

## 4 — Feel: actuators, disc brakes, ABS, no flips

Actuator ramps (measured, Aurora V12): steering to full lock **0.133 s**, throttle to WOT
**0.300 s**, brake to full pressure **0.183 s**. Self-centring (5.4 rad/s) is faster than turn-in
(3.1 rad/s at rest, 1.35 at the steering limit speed), and the available lock still falls off with
speed.

Braking from 100 km/h on the flat plane, both cars:

| car | distance | time | deceleration | ABS steps | peak chassis tilt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aurora V12 (1180 kg, `brakeGain` 1.5) | 35.7 m | 2.48 s | **1.10 g** | 4 / 149 | 2° |
| Sikandar SUV (2200 kg, `brakeGain` 1.9) | 39.7 m | 2.78 s | **0.99 g** | 4 / 167 | 2° |

The heavier SUV stops in a comparable distance (ratio 1.11) instead of needing twice the pedal
travel — that is what `brakeGain` is for, since `serviceBrakeForce` is a pinned per-car reference
figure (8500 N and 11000 N) and not a deceleration.

The units bug is asserted directly: the per-wheel brake value equals
`serviceBrakeForce × brakeGain × frontBias × dt / 2`, i.e. an impulse cap in N·s. Passing raw
newtons (M2) was 60× that, which is why one frame of `S` from 60 km/h stopped the car dead, dove
the nose and unloaded the rear.

Rollover guard: 300 steps of full braking from > 110 km/h while alternating full lock every second
— **peak tilt < 1.2 rad, zero flips, for both cars**. Roll and pitch rates are clamped in the
chassis frame (2.3 rad/s in contact, 3.22 airborne) while **yaw is left untouched**, so a drift
still spins freely. A car tipped to 0.95 rad and dropped recovers to < 0.5 rad in **~2 s**
(120 steps Aurora, 136 SUV) and drives away; `control.flipped` clears and `control.rollovers`
counts the excursion.

Drift mode at 70 km/h, half lock for 2 s: **2.99 rad of yaw**, still 71 km/h, **peak tilt 1°**.

**Not a regression, but worth recording:** 0–100 km/h takes 23.0 s (Aurora) / 24.5 s (SUV) and both
cars pull 106.8 / 103.3 km/h against presets of 165 / 148. Measured against `HEAD` (M2.5) the
numbers are *identical*, so M2.6 did not change them — the preset `maxForwardForce` figures
(2050 N on 1180 kg, 4400 N on 2200 kg) are simply gentle, and `engineFade` plus `linearDamping`
0.035 set the equilibrium well below `topSpeedKmh`. Rebalancing the engines was not in the M2.6
brief; it is the obvious first item for M2.7.

## 5 / 6 — P/R/N/D and drift

`shift({ selector })`: **P** and **N** return gear 0 with `drive: false` and a throttle-only
free-rev (idle off throttle, limiter at 92% of redline on, road speed ignored); **R** returns −1;
**D** never returns −1, including at −12 km/h, which is the whole "S in D only brakes" fix. An
unknown or missing selector falls back to D; `engineLoad()` in P/N is < 0.2 at 6000 rpm so a parked
blip sounds empty rather than loaded.

Keys **1–4** select P/R/N/D with **P** and **N** as aliases (`R` stays Reset, `D` stays steer-right —
both long documented). The gate latches onto `physics.control`, survives `resetVehicle()` and a car
swap, and is reflected on both the HUD pill and the on-screen gate. Touch gets a tappable gate plus
a **held** handbrake (`data-control="handbrake"`) and a **latched** drift toggle
(`data-action="drift"`), which phones had no equivalent of at all.

## 7 — Forest: ground-only collision, cleaned heights, tree colliders, Free Roam

Classification of all 26 materials in the shipped scene (`tests/forest-collision.test.js`):

| class | materials |
| --- | --- |
| **terrain** (the only class sampled into the heightfield) | Dirt_Road, Dirt_Road_Bare, Dirt_Road_Trails, Ground_Dirt, Road_Edge_Gravel_Dusty, Mud_Pile, Cobblestone, Puddle_Streaks, Sloped_Rock, Tall_Cliff, Broken_Rocks, Rock_Decal |
| vegetation | Background_Tree_Atlas, Forest_Bush, Grass_Close, Aerial_Grass, Grass_Vegetation_Green/Dry, Fallen_Maple_Leaves, Fallen_Generic_Leaves |
| trunk (own colliders) | Trunk_Oak, Trunk_Birch |
| prop | Wood_Fence, Metal_Fence, Wood_Log |
| backdrop | Terrain_Far |

The M2 pattern matched `tree|trunk|bush|grass|leaves|aerial|forest|vegetation`, and the atlas is
named `Background_Tree_Atlas` — so *all 26* classified as ground, nothing was ever skipped
(`SKIP_PATTERN` is guarded by `!GROUND_PATTERN.test(label)`), and the canopy, trunk atlas and
standing grass cards were all sampled as the driveable surface.

Cleaning the **shipped** `forest-dirt-trail.terrain.json` (263 × 274 cells, 2 m) at load:

| | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cells more than 8 m from their 8-neighbour median | **150** | **3** |
| sentinel cells (unreachable, written as a bare 0 → a 96 m pit) | 28 | 0 |
| single-cell spikes / holes corrected | — | 67 / 70 |
| over-steep cells slope-limited (the `Terrain_Far` seam, ~38 m in one cell) | — | 197 |
| max neighbour-to-neighbour step | 100.4 m | 38.6 m, and only at the map edge 83 m off the road |

The filter is *relative* (median absolute deviation of the 8 neighbours), which is what keeps real
terrain: a genuine cliff has most of its neighbours spread across it too. The suite asserts a
synthetic cliff survives, a smooth rolling grid is untouched, and an adjacent spike/hole *pair* is
both removed — a max-min spread test would let the two grant each other immunity.

The cleaner lives in `src/game/tracks/terrain-grid.js` and is **imported by both sides** (the
pipeline re-exports it), so the bake and the runtime cannot disagree about what a spike is.

Tree trunks, recovered from the decoded shipped GLB by clustering trunk-atlas vertices on a 2.4 m
XZ grid:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| trunks found | **128** (137 at 3.2 m, 73 at 1.2 m) in ~1 ms |
| radius min / median / max | 0.39 / **0.54** / 1.16 m |
| collider height min / median / max | 7.9 / **12.4** / 18.0 m |
| inside the Free Roam extent | 128 of 128 |
| static bodies (24 m tiles) | **56**, holding 128 cannon `Cylinder` shapes |

Trunks are grouped into tiles rather than one body each because every wheel raycast tests each
body's AABB. A trunk standing inside the road corridor is dropped as a bake artifact, and each
collider is seated on the *cleaned* heightfield rather than on the mesh's own lowest vertex.

Corridor: the traced road half-width is **12.04 m** and the M2 clamp cut it to 9.5 m, putting the
soft walls inside the visible verges. The clamp is now 13.5 m, so the trail is its real width.

**Forest Free Roam** (`forest-free-roam`) is the same scene and the same collider — one shared
download, one heightfield, refcounted disposal — with the corridor spring replaced by a soft outer
edge 14 m inside the heightfield boundary (a 524 × 546 m map). `bounds.isOnTrack` still means "on
the dirt road", so off-road grip is unchanged; switching between the two forest modes is instant.

## 8 — Cameras and the Aurora cockpit

Orbit engages at any speed (`ORBIT_MAX_KMH` is `Infinity`; the constant survives as the speed below
which the first orbit frame snaps instead of smoothing) and no longer hands control back above a
threshold. The drag now sets an angle **relative to the car's heading**: driven through a 90°
left-hander at ~75 km/h from a three-quarter view, the relative bearing moves by **< 0.08 rad**
while the world bearing swings right round with the car (`tests/cameras.test.js`). In the plane the
orbit target follows the car exactly — smoothing X/Z would leave the camera trailing, and in a
corner the lag grows with the centripetal acceleration — and only the vertical axis is filtered,
which is where the suspension bounce actually is.

Aurora cockpit corrections, declared per car in `preset.cockpit`:

| | baked estimate | M2.6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| head position | `[-0.38, 0.760, -0.315]` | **+85 mm** → `y ≈ 0.845` (roof at 0.912, so still inside) |
| steering wheel radius | 0.170 m | **0.142 m**, moved −45 mm in y and +35 mm in z |
| roof / cabin | none — sky showed through the canopy | dark shell (`#14161a`, `BackSide`, `envMapIntensity` 0.18), **shown only in the cockpit camera** |

The SUV has a real modelled cabin (`steering_dummy` is found by name), so it only gets +20 mm of eye
height and a 0.19 m rim; `darkInterior` is off. A real wheel node is rescaled to the declared rim
radius (measured in the pivot's own frame, clamped 0.55–1.7×), so the correction works whether the
wheel is a node, a material match or procedural.

## What must land via CI

Two fixes are code/config changes that only take effect when the asset pipeline re-runs, and the
source host is not reachable from this sandbox:

1. **`public/tracks/forest-dirt-trail.terrain.json`** — re-baking with the ground-only
   classification, the shared outlier filter and `extractTrunks()` writes `trunks`,
   `groundMaterials` / `excludedMaterials` and `stats.{spikes,holes,filled,unreachable,trunks}`.
   Until then the runtime filter and the scene-derived trunk list carry the load, which is exactly
   what they were built to do.
2. **`public/models/sikandar-suv.glb`** — re-encoding at q85.

`npm run assets:pipeline` runs both; the workflow commits the result and its log to
`assets/reports/pipeline.log`.

## Checklist for the browser pass

Things that cannot be evidenced headlessly:

- [ ] Network waterfall: car GLB requested before the HDRI; the generated sky is on screen while it loads
- [ ] Horizon haze is blue-warm, not white; no bloom on the distance; forest dimmer than circuit
- [ ] Switch Aurora → SUV → Aurora with sound on: the diesel is audible, and it is a *different* engine
- [ ] Lift at 7000 rpm: overrun/burble. Shift: an audible dip. Idle in P: a free-rev to the limiter
- [ ] Tap `A` for one frame: the car nudges, it does not dart. Hold `S` from 100 km/h: progressive, no nose-dive flip
- [ ] `1`–`4` move the gate; `S` in D brakes only; `R` reverses; `P` holds on a slope
- [ ] `T` / MODE loosens the rear; the held DRIFT button rotates the car on a phone
- [ ] Orbit while driving, drag to a three-quarter view, take a corner: the car stays framed
- [ ] Cockpit in the Aurora: eyes above the dash, a small low wheel, a dark roof — no sky
- [ ] Forest: the car is not thrown by the surface; a tree stops it; Free Roam turns you back softly at the map edge
- [ ] SUV textures at q85 after the pipeline re-run
